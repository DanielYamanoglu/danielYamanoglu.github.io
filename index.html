about

Control theory meets applied AI

I started in Control & Automation Engineering, where I studied feedback loops, state estimation and signal processing. That background shapes how I approach AI systems: I define what to measure, inspect failure modes and make system behaviour easier to understand.

My graduation thesis, an EKF-integrated fuzzy computed-torque controller for a 2-DOF robot manipulator, trained me to connect mathematical models with noisy signals. I bring the same habit to retrieval and agent systems: make data flow, state and evaluation explicit.

Right now I focus on RAG, knowledge graphs and agentic workflows. I connect language models to tools, memory and structured data, then test how the system behaves when retrieval or a tool call fails.
